# Salamander

![Salamander](Salamander.png)

## Motivation

At the 2026 FSAE competition NER, among many other teams, was disqualified due to non-functional energy meter temperature sensors in our Tractive Battery. Prior to this competition no team had any way to test whether or not their temperature sensors would work before going all the way through EV tech and putting the actual energy meter in the car. Enter Salamander; this PCB allows NER to communicate with the DS18B20 temperature sensors and ensure that they are functional before going out to Michigan.

## Technical Specifications

* STM32G0 MCU
* Galvanically isolated communications
* I2C-OneWire converter for ease of use

Salamander features a 4 layer stackup which helps to break out all the pins on the MCU, FTDI, and power priority circuits.
